What each setting means

Scenario: the network demand and timed events.
Controller: the traffic-light decision rule.
Seed: reproducible random traffic.
Demand multiplier: scale the existing vehicle flows.
Preemption: emergency-vehicle signal priority.
Duration: optional end time in simulated seconds; the first 900 s are warm-up and are not measured.
